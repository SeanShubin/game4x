## Scope

- A single planet
- Tiny, which is 12 territories

## The loop

1. No presence on the planet
2. Colonize a single territory on the planet
3. Create infrastructure to grow food to support population growth
4. Expand population across the planet
5. Create infrastructure to mine metal and fuel from the planet
6. Create infrastructure to build a new colonizer
7. Create infrastructure to launch the colonizer into space
8. Launch the colonizer into space
