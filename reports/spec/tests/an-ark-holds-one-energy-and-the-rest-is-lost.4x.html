Three energy in an orbit with one ark, a turn ends, and one is left.

Sean, 2026-09-20: *the ark can only store 1 energy so excess is wasted, the sun may have a
density higher than 1, also wasted because the ark can't store it.* Two of the three go, and
nothing new does it: energy is loose, the ark is the only thing in an orbit that holds any, and
discard-disorder takes what will not fit.

Where the three came from is not this test's business. an-ark-gathers-what-the-sun-gives is
where a sun of density three hands them over; here they are simply standing, because what an ark
can take and what an ark can keep are two different numbers and each deserves its own world.

A unit is the container, which is the transport's twelve in miniature - rooming counts the
arks and multiplies, the same arithmetic that sums a transport's two rows for fuel. It is why
answering fuel is the same thing as energy cost nothing to build.

The ark is not storing the energy. spec/logistics.md is plain that a resource in a place is
in that place and not in a container inside it: the things in it that can hold that kind
contribute capacity and hold nothing. Take the ark away and all three would go, because a loose
kind nothing gives room for has no room.
