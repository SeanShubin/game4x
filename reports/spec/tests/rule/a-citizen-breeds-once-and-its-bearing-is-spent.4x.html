A citizen that breeds comes back unable to breed again, and the child can.

Sean, 2026-09-28: *that is a limiter so that one citizen can't have multiple children, its
like labor running an extractor with citizen as the output, so I would expect it to be load
bearing.* Until P-587 it was not: breed added two citizens in one clause at bearing:1,
so the parent came back able to breed again and the only bound was food.

toil is the pattern and breed now matches it. A citizen is required with its trait, the
row is taken, and it is put back spent - so the turn's end is what restores it and
{refresh what:citizen trait:bearing} has work to do.

Two food and one citizen with a bearing. If the parent could breed again the second food would
be eaten and there would be three citizens; there are two, and one food is left over. The
leftover food is what makes this a test of the limiter rather than of arithmetic - the rule was
able to fire again and the world refused it.

repeats is still on breed and is not what caps this. It means *fire for every citizen that
can*, and after the first firing this citizen cannot.
