Twenty-two metal where there is room for twenty, and an extractor is built out of it anyway.

This is why disorder is not a refusal. Sean, 2026-09-19: *i am going to have mechanics that
allow for stuff over capacity to be used in other recipies to avoid the waste.* spec/resources.md
had already said it: *Matter that is out of its source may be spent, whether it is in disorder or
in order.*

build-extractor says nothing about disorder and does not have to. It removes one labor and
one metal, and the metal it removes is the territory's - there is no such thing as *a metal in
disorder to name, only a place holding more than fits. *That is the whole of what Sean meant by
not tracking what is stored where.

Twenty-two becomes twenty-one, which is still over the twenty there is room for - so spending
it once does not make the rest orderly, and the turn still takes what is left over.
