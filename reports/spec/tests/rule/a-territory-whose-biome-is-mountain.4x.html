A territory whose biome is mountain, and a command that does not touch it.

spec/planet.md: Each territory has a biome, and *a biome earns its place by being shown
rather than by being obeyed.* So what there is to assert is that a terrain can be stated and
that no rule disturbs it - which is shown rather than obeyed said as a test.

terrain is the join and biome is the vocabulary. spec/data/schema.4x: *biome is
vocabulary and terrain is state, which is why only the second carries a {state} row - the
six do not change while a game runs, and which territory has which is the world.*

One test per biome, because the vocabulary is six facts. spec/planet.md: *the biomes are
ocean, ice, desert, grassland, jungle and mountain.* The mutation sweep found all six rows with
nothing referring to any of them - C-218 - and six tests of four rows is what makes all six
load-bearing. A single test naming all six is not available: a fourth {terrain} row is
refused even with its territory declared above it, which is S-247.

The command is a refresh because it has to fire and must not be about terrain. A test where
nothing happens would pass against an engine that cannot run at all; this moves a scout's
moving and leaves the terrain alone, so the {then} asserts rather than copies.

{test name:a-territory-whose-biome-is-mountain}

{given}
{territory id:1 name:territory-1}
{place id:1 of:territory-1 layer:surface name:place-1}
{terrain of:territory-1 is:mountain}
{scout where:place-1 moving:0} -> 1

{when}
{refresh what:unit trait:moving}

{then}
{territory id:1 name:territory-1}
{place id:1 of:territory-1 layer:surface name:place-1}
{terrain of:territory-1 is:mountain}
{scout where:place-1 moving:1} -> 1
