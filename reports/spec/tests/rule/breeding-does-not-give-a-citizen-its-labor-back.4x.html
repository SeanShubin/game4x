A citizen that has spent its labour still has it spent after it breeds.

This is the twin of the bearing defect and was found by following Sean's own analogy. Before
P-587, breed's one add asserted laboring:1 for both citizens while its remove did not
constrain laboring at all - so a citizen that had already toiled bred and came back able to toil
again. Free labour, once per food.

It was never an infinite-resource glitch, which reports/nogain.md decides rather than
assumes: the solver found a weighting with that rule in place. It was a behaviour nobody
asserted, which is the other thing a rule nothing depends on can be.

The parent's labour is read rather than stated now - `{reading id:17 clause:clause-27
column:137 of:clause-25 takes:137} - which is how upkeep` has always carried a value through.
The child is stated at 1, because everything made arrives at full capability.
