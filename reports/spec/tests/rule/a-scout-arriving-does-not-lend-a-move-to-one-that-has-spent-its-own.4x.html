A spent scout stands where a fresh one arrives, and the two do not pool their moves.

Sean, 2026-09-18: *or allow a scout that has moved on a previous turn to pick up a move from
a scout on the place it moved to.* The arriving scout spends its move getting there, so the
territory holds two scouts and no moves.

This is why the allowance is a key column. Were it not, the two scouts would be one row of
two and the move left in the group would be anybody's.

{given}
{territory id:1 name:territory-1}
{place id:1 of:territory-1 layer:surface name:place-1}
{territory id:2 name:territory-2}
{place id:2 of:territory-2 layer:surface name:place-2}
{territory id:3 name:territory-3}
