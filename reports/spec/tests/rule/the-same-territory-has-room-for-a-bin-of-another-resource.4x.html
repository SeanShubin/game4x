The same territory, the same full metal capacity, and a food bin goes up anyway.

This is the whole point of the increment. Sean, 2026-09-17: *Each territory will have a limit
of how many storage structures of each type it can hold.* Of each type is what this says and
what a-bin-cannot-be-built-where-the-capacity-is-taken cannot say on its own - a cap that was
counted per territory would refuse this too, and the pair is what tells the two apart.

It needed no mechanism. A capacity names a what, so the metal bins and the food ones are
counted against different questions without anything being said about resources.

The metal is spent on a food bin, which is what a bin costs rather than what it holds:
building is one labor and one metal whatever the bin is for.
