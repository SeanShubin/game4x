Two scouts with a move and three without, refreshed, and five with a move afterwards.

A put is why this is one row and not two. Refreshing assigns moving:1 to every group, so
the spent three join the fresh two - and the group that was already topped off is left alone
rather than refused, which is the whole reason the role exists.

Sean, 2026-09-18: *This is not a transformation recipe, it is creation. It is similar in
nature to something generated by time. The resources that come from the ground and sun are
infinite but rate limited. So are moves.*

{test name:refresh-makes-one-entry-of-a-spent-scout-and-a-fresh-one}

{given}
{territory id:1 name:territory-1}
{place id:1 of:territory-1 layer:surface name:place-1}
{scout where:place-1 moving:1} -> 2
