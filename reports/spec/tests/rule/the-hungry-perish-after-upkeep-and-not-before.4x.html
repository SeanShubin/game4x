Three citizens and two food here, two and one there, and one turn leaves three citizens.

Swap upkeep and perish in the turn and the answer is none. perish fired first would
find all five citizens hungry and take all five, and the food would go untouched - so this is the
first test whose answer is carried by part.seq rather than by which rules there are.

That is what the order of the turn is for. Sean, 2026-09-18: *it must be able to organize the
entirety of game rules is some type of acyclic graph or tree.* Eating before starving is a fact
about the tree, written once where a player can read it in tree.txt.

Two territories, because perish is scoped and one would not show it. Its clause binds the
place it acts in; take that binding away and the pattern matches a hungry citizen in either
territory, which is two rows and no way to say which - so the rule is refused rather than
guessing, and with one territory there would be nothing to guess between.

The survivors are hungry again, and there is no way to see them otherwise. The turn's last
step restores every count, and nothing groups the eating and the starving into something that can
be fired without it. That is what dropping adjust-population cost - the composite was a
seam, and it was the only one.
