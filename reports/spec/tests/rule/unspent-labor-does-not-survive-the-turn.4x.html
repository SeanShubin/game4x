Five labour, one of them spent on an extractor, and the other four gone when the turn ends.

Sean, 2026-09-20: unspent labor does not survive the turn and *excess labor to be removed
the same as discard-disorder.* So there is no rule for it: labour is loose, nothing in the world
gives room for labour, and a loose kind nothing gives room for has no room - so all of it is
in disorder and the turn takes it.

No capacity row says nothing holds labour. The absence of one does, which is the rule Sean
chose over stating a zero: *if we omit a capacity, we can default that to mean it may carry none
of that thing.* It had been written down and deferred for want of a user since 2026-09-19.

One labour is spent so that the five are worth stating. A first draft simply put labour in the
world and ended the turn, and the mutation sweep said the row could be deleted: a world where the
labour was taken and a world that never had any are the same world afterwards, so nothing could
tell them apart. What is destroyed leaves no trace, so the only evidence is what was built with
what survived long enough to be spent.

How many were left over cannot be seen, and that is the point rather than a gap - four or
forty, they all go.
