Two hungry citizens and a fed one here with one food, one citizen and five food there, and
neither the surplus nor the meal travels.

This is what the scope buys. upkeep takes a citizen and a food from the same territory,
which is the join every other rule makes through an argument - and a world-owned rule has no
caller to hand it one. The engine fires it once per territory instead.

One citizen here is left hungry with five food a territory away, which is the whole point: a
surplus is local until something moves it, and nothing here moves food.

The fed citizen is in the given and not an outcome, which is the other thing this asserts.
Two citizen rows stand in one place, and remove a citizen would match both - so the clause says
hungry:1, and the one that has already eaten is not fed twice. Take that away and the rule is
refused for not having said which, rather than feeding whichever row came first.
