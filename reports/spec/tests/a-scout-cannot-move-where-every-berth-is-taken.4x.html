Six berths of six are taken and a seventh berth's worth has nowhere to stand.

No rule mentions berths. move puts the scout down and the world it would leave consumes
seven where six are provided, which is where the refusal comes from - so this binds every rule
that puts a vehicle anywhere, including ones not written yet.
