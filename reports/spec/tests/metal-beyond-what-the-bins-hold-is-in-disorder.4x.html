Two bins hold twenty metal, and twenty-two is a world that exists.

Over capacity is not refused; it is disorder. Sean, 2026-09-19: *We will be able to have
things in a territory over capacity, and everything over capacity is in disorder. There will be
an end turn mechanic that discards everything in disorder. *This test used to assert the
opposite - that twenty-two metal could not exist - and it was the intent that was wrong rather
than the arithmetic.

{loose kind:resource} is what makes the difference, and structures do not have it: a third
bin where there is room for two is still refused, which
a-bin-cannot-be-built-where-the-capacity-is-taken states. Sean: *structures are intentionally
built for the purpose of having that structure there, while resources are mined to be spent or
stored.*

Sixteen and six is twenty-two, against room for twenty - so two are in disorder, and
ending-a-turn-takes-what-is-in-disorder is what happens to them.
