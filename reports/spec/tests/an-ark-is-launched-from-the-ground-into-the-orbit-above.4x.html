A labour, a metal and an energy on the ground, and an ark in the orbit above.

Sean, 2026-09-20: *building/launching an ark will be one action, removing the resources
required to build an ark from the surface and adding an ark to orbit. And on the cost: a labor
to assemble it, materials to build it, energy to launch it - each of them one, because for now I
am making everything cheap.*

Nothing crosses between layers, and this is not a crossing. The ark is not lifted from the
ground; it is made, and where a made thing appears is the rule's to say. A transformation rather
than a journey, which is why move can forbid crossing without forbidding this.

The orbit is worked out and not named. The command says only which surface place is building.
The rule reads that place's territory and asks for the one place that has it and is an orbit -
found by matching, so a territory with no orbit above it simply cannot launch.

A yard is required and is not spent, so it is in the given and still in the then. That is
the clause the migration dropped and P-583 put back: spec/structures.md says *a Yard produces
Arks*, and producing is not consuming. It is state rather than a story - spec/README.md rule
3 - so it is given rather than built here, and a-yard-is-built-from-labor-and-metal says what
building one costs.

The ark arrives able to move and to gather, which is what everything made here does. Sean,
2026-09-20: Generally i want things created with full capability. So it may turn to the sun on
the turn it was built.
