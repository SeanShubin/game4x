Two deposits in one territory, and working one of them leaves the other exactly as it was.

This is the parameterization. what is an argument like any other, so one rule serves every
resource - and what says so is that the metal deposit is untouched while the food one pays out.

{test name:a-food-extractor-and-a-metal-extractor-do-not-interfere}

{given}
{territory id:1 name:territory-1}
{place id:1 of:territory-1 layer:surface name:place-1}
{capacity of:deposit for:extractor what:resource per:place} -> 1
{deposit where:place-1 what:food density:6} -> 1
{deposit where:place-1 what:metal density:2} -> 1
{extractor where:place-1 what:food working:1} -> 1
{extractor where:place-1 what:metal working:1} -> 1
{labor where:place-1} -> 1

{when}
{work where:place-1 what:food}

{then}
{territory id:1 name:territory-1}
{place id:1 of:territory-1 layer:surface name:place-1}
{capacity of:deposit for:extractor what:resource per:place} -> 1
{deposit where:place-1 what:food density:6} -> 1
{deposit where:place-1 what:metal density:2} -> 1
{extractor where:place-1 what:food working:0} -> 1
{extractor where:place-1 what:metal working:1} -> 1
{food where:place-1} -> 6
