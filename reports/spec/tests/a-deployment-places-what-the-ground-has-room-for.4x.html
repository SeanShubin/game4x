Metal in the ground and no food, and the deployment places the metal extractor and not the food
one.

Two soft lines and they do not share a fate. Each takes the room there is where it would
stand, and a capacity names one resource - so what holds metal extractors says nothing about food
ones. This is the test that says softness is per line, which the barren one and the settled
one cannot say between them.

Nothing here is a choice the engine made. The metal line asks for one and there is room for
one; the food line asks for one and there is room for none. A soft line has no preference and no
order - it takes what fits and the rest of the rule goes on.

The settlement it leaves cannot feed itself, which is the point of the whole mechanism rather
than a defect in it. Sean, 2026-09-20: *consequences of legal moves may be disastrous, but that is
player choice.*
