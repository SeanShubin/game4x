An extractor works its deposit and produces the deposit's density, not one.

This is the whole point of a deposit. Every other rule produces a number written into the
rule; this one produces a number read out of the world, which is what spec/data/line.4x writes
as $where's density for that resource.

{test name:an-extractor-pulls-its-deposits-density}

{given}
{territory id:1 name:territory-1}
{place id:1 of:territory-1 layer:surface name:place-1}
{capacity of:deposit for:extractor what:resource per:place} -> 1
{deposit where:place-1 what:food density:6} -> 1
{extractor where:place-1 what:food working:1} -> 1
{labor where:place-1} -> 1

{when}
{work where:place-1 what:food}

{then}
{territory id:1 name:territory-1}
{place id:1 of:territory-1 layer:surface name:place-1}
{capacity of:deposit for:extractor what:resource per:place} -> 1
{deposit where:place-1 what:food density:6} -> 1
{extractor where:place-1 what:food working:0} -> 1
{food where:place-1} -> 6
