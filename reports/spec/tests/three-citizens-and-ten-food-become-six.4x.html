The whole loop in one turn: three citizens and ten food become six citizens and four food.

This is Sean's core loop, 2026-09-19: *citizen works food extractor / food extractor generates
enough food for more than one citizen / each citizen cosumes 1 food or perishes / each remaining
(citizen, food) produces an additional citizen.* The first two are work, which this test does
not fire; the last two are the turn.

Three eaten and three bred is six taken from ten. upkeep fires three times and stops when
the citizens run out, and breed fires three times and stops for the same reason - there is food
for seven more and nobody left to bear.

Nothing computes the six. Each firing takes one citizen and one food and gives two citizens
back, and how many times it fired is the whole of the arithmetic.

And the four food that are left over are gone, because nothing in this world holds food. A
loose kind nothing gives room for has no room, so all of it is in disorder and the turn takes it.
Surplus feeds the next generation only if there is a bin to keep it in - which is storage
earning its place in the game rather than being a thing you may build.

The survivors are fertile and hungry again, because the turn restores every count last - so
the world is in the shape it started in, with twice the citizens and less food.
