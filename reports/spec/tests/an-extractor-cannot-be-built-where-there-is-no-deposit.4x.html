A territory with the labor and the metal but no deposit of that resource at all.

A deposit that is full and a deposit that is not there are one refusal. A row at quantity
zero is never written - Sean, 2026-09-15: the model is a minimal expression of intent - so
having no room and having no deposit differ only in the number, and the refusal names the row
that would have had to exist either way.
