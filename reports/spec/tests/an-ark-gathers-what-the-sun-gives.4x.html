A sun of density three and one ark: three energy.

Sean, 2026-09-20: *think of the sun as providing unlimited capacity for extractors, and the
ark as an extractor for solar energy.* So the sun is a deposit of energy standing in the orbit,
and gather reads its density exactly as work reads a deposit's - what an ark takes is what the
sun gives, and the number is the world's rather than the rule's.

An ark is not an extractor. {capacity of:deposit for:extractor ...} counts extractors
against deposits and an ark counts against nothing, which is where *unlimited capacity for
extractors* comes from: not a number, but the ark not being one of them.

Three is gathered and three is still standing, because nothing here ends a turn. What an ark
can keep is a different question and an-ark-holds-one-energy-and-the-rest-is-lost is where it is
asked - the two numbers are not the same and that is the point.

Its moving is read back rather than stated. An add must name every column, and writing
moving:1 would hand a spent move back for free to an ark that had already crossed.
