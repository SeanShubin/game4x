A bin costs one labor and one metal, the same as an extractor, and stands where it was built.

build-bin is the second rule of that shape and says nothing about capacity, the way
build-extractor says nothing about deposits: the world it would leave either fits or it does
not, and every rule already refuses one that does not.

The capacity of four is on the page and only one bin is built, so this test says what
building costs and the two beside it say what the cap does. Sean, 2026-09-17: *I need see
what the actual numbers are, not some hidden default.*
