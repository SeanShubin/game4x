The scout moves twice, one step at a time, because territory 1 does not touch territory 3.

A second test, added by adding a file. Nothing in tests/ or src/ names it.

The turn between them is not decoration. A scout has one move, so crossing two borders in
one go is exactly what the allowance forbids - and this test would have been the first casualty
of that rule. It now says what crossing twice costs, and the cost is a turn.

This is the multi-turn shape. Sean, 2026-09-18: *it will make it easier to read tests across
multiple turns if i see an end turn command.* It said {refresh ...} here while refresh was
something a player could choose; a player cannot, so what stands between the two moves is the
thing that actually stands between them.
