An ark is launched from a yard, so a territory with everything else and no yard launches nothing.

spec/structures.md: A Yard produces Arks. This is the clause the migration dropped.
The old ruleset's launch ark recipe carried require 1 yard beside its territory requirement;
the rule in spec/data/rules.4x requires two places and removes a labor, a metal and an energy,
and names no yard at all.

It is the sibling of an-ark-cannot-launch-where-there-is-no-orbit, and the pair is the whole
of what launching needs that is not a resource: somewhere to build it, and somewhere for it to go.

The labor, metal and energy are all present on purpose. A test that left one of them out would
pass whether or not the yard were required, because the rule would refuse it for the missing
resource - so the refusal named here is the only one it can be.
