Twenty-two metal where there is room for twenty, a turn ends, and twenty are left.

discard-disorder is the turn's second part, after the population is adjusted - which is
the order spec/turn.md states: upkeep, then growth or starvation, then *what expires expires,
and what was not kept in order is lost*, then what nature reclaims, and time restoring the counts
last. Only what nature reclaims is missing, and the tree in tree.txt is where the order can
be read.

Nothing says which two metal went. A place holds one number for a kind, so there is no metal
in disorder to pick out - the number is bounded and what is above the bound is gone. Sean,
2026-09-17: *we only lose what we don't have the storage for, without tracking what is stored
where.*

Every other step does nothing here. Nothing in this world is a citizen, so adjusting the
population finds nobody to feed and nobody to take; and nothing carries a moving or a working,
so the refreshes are put matching nothing. That is why the whole turn leaves the world one
step of it leaves, and it is what makes this and discarding-disorder-can-be-fired-on-its-own a
pair rather than a duplicate.
