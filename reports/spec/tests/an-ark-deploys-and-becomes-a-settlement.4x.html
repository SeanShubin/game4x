settlement.

Sean, 2026-09-20, whole: *ark can deploy to the surface, which entails destruction of the ark,
along with creation of 1 metal extractor, 1 food extractor, and 2 citizens.*

The ground is found by matching, the same way the orbit is when an ark launches. The command
names the orbit the ark is in; the rule reads that place's territory and asks for the one place
that has it and is a surface. What would be an equality test is a pattern, which is the move
this engine makes everywhere a comparison would do.

Nothing crossed a layer. The ark was removed from orbit and what it became was added to the
ground - a transformation rather than a journey, which is what lets
nothing-moves-between-the-layers forbid crossing without forbidding this.

The two extractors fit because there is a deposit for each. They are soft lines, and a soft
line that has room takes all of it - so this test says nothing about softness that
a-deployment-with-nowhere-to-mine-still-costs-the-ark does not say better.

The citizens arrive at full capability - bearing:1, laboring:1, and hungry:0, which is
the same thing said backwards because a citizen that owes no meal is a citizen that has eaten.
Sean, 2026-09-20: Generally i want things created with full capability.

So do the extractors, at working:1, and either may be worked the turn the ark comes down.
