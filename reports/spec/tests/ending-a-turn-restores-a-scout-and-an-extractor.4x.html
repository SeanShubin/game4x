Ending a turn restores two kinds of count, because it has two parts and they both fire.

This is the composite, and the two tests beside it are its parts.
refresh-makes-one-entry-of-a-spent-scout-and-a-fresh-one is what `{refresh what:scout
trait:moving} does on its own; this is what end-turn` does with it. Sean, 2026-09-18: *it will
be easier to test the end turn command itself if i can test its parts.*

A scout and an extractor, because the two parts carry different traits. The scout's moving
comes back from part one and the extractor's working from part two, so a test with only one
kind in it would pass with either part deleted.

Each kind starts with a spent group and a fresh one, so the two join and the count is the
sum: two scouts and one, three; one extractor and one, two. The first draft started both kinds
wholly spent and the mutation sweep named both values as reading nothing - changing either
working:0 or moving:0 to 1 made the turn a no-op and the then a copy of the given, and
the test passed having restored nothing. This is the same slack, in the same shape, one test
after it was fixed in one-refresh-reaches-a-scout-and-a-transport.

The deposit holds two because two extractors stand in it - the room a deposit gives is summed
over the extractors standing in it whatever state they are in, and a world with more of them than
there is room for is refused. Nothing here has to say so, because this test states no capacity
for extractors at all and a world that says nothing bounds nothing.

{test name:ending-a-turn-restores-a-scout-and-an-extractor}

{given}
{territory id:1 name:territory-1}
{place id:1 of:territory-1 layer:surface name:place-1}
{capacity of:deposit for:extractor what:resource per:place} -> 1
{deposit where:place-1 what:food density:6} -> 2
{extractor where:place-1 what:food working:0} -> 1
{extractor where:place-1 what:food working:1} -> 1
{scout where:place-1 moving:0} -> 2
{scout where:place-1 moving:1} -> 1

{when}
{end-turn}

{then}
{territory id:1 name:territory-1}
{place id:1 of:territory-1 layer:surface name:place-1}
{capacity of:deposit for:extractor what:resource per:place} -> 1
{deposit where:place-1 what:food density:6} -> 2
{extractor where:place-1 what:food working:1} -> 2
{scout where:place-1 moving:1} -> 3
