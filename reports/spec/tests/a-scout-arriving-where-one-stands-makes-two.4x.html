The index · the file itself, as text

A scout moving to where a scout already stands makes two of them.

This is the add half on its own. One leaves a territory that held one, so the row going is
right either way - what this asks is whether arriving joined what was there.

{test name:a-scout-arriving-where-one-stands-makes-two}

{given}
{territory id:1 name:territory-1}
{place id:1 of:territory-1 layer:surface name:place-1}
{territory id:2 name:territory-2}
{place id:2 of:territory-2 layer:surface name:place-2}
{adjacency id:1 from:territory-1 to:territory-2}
{scout where:place-1 moving:1} -> 1
{scout where:place-2 moving:0} -> 1

{when}
{move what:scout from:place-1 to:place-2}

{then}
{territory id:1 name:territory-1}
{place id:1 of:territory-1 layer:surface name:place-1}
{territory id:2 name:territory-2}
{place id:2 of:territory-2 layer:surface name:place-2}
{adjacency id:1 from:territory-1 to:territory-2}
{scout where:place-2 moving:0} -> 2
