Three citizens, two food, a turn ends, and two citizens are left.

This is the core loop Sean stated, 2026-09-19: each citizen cosumes 1 food or perishes.
Nothing here computes a minimum - upkeep fires as many times as it can, and it can fire twice
because there are two food. The third citizen is still hungry when perish runs.

The survivors are hungry again at the end, because the turn's last step restores every count
the same way it restores a scout's move. So a citizen that ate this turn must eat again next
turn, and the world is back in the shape it started in.

The food is gone rather than at zero. An entry is never zero - spec/console.md - so a row
taken down to nothing is not written.
