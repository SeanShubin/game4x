Building an extractor spends a labor and a metal, and puts the extractor in a deposit.

The deposit is untouched. It is not spent - it is what holds the extractor, and holding one
is what it goes on doing. What changes is that one of its three is now occupied, which the world
says by there being an extractor row rather than by the deposit counting down.
