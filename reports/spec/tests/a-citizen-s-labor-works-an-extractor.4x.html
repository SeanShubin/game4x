The loop, closed: a citizen makes a labour, the labour works a food extractor, and one food in
becomes six out.

This is the half of Sean's loop that had no mechanism until now. *citizen works food extractor
/ food extractor generates enough food for more than one citizen* - and labor was consumed by
three rules and produced by none, so every test that spent one stated it in a given. A
population could grow and starve while nothing it did made the food it lived on.

Both halves are the player's. Sean, 2026-09-20: *I was expecting citizens to generate labor
during a regular turn.* So toil is a command and not a step of the turn - working is a choice,
and a citizen that is not put to work keeps its laboring until the turn restores it anyway.

One command works the whole place, because toil repeats: it fires once per citizen that
still has its laboring and stops when none has. Here that is one citizen and one labour.

The extractor is spent afterwards and the labour is gone, so nothing in this world can work
again until a turn restores the one and a citizen makes the other.

{test name:a-citizen-s-labor-works-an-extractor}

{given}
{territory id:1 name:territory-1}
{place id:1 of:territory-1 layer:surface name:place-1}
{capacity of:deposit for:extractor what:resource per:place} -> 1
{citizen where:place-1 hungry:1 bearing:1 laboring:1} -> 1
{deposit where:place-1 what:food density:6} -> 1
{extractor where:place-1 what:food working:1} -> 1

{when}
{toil where:place-1}
{work where:place-1 what:food}

{then}
{territory id:1 name:territory-1}
{place id:1 of:territory-1 layer:surface name:place-1}
{capacity of:deposit for:extractor what:resource per:place} -> 1
{citizen where:place-1 hungry:1 bearing:1 laboring:0} -> 1
{deposit where:place-1 what:food density:6} -> 1
{extractor where:place-1 what:food working:0} -> 1
{food where:place-1} -> 6
