Two fed citizens and five food, breeding fired on its own: four citizens and three food.

It stops at two with three food standing, and the food is the evidence. Were the newborns
reached, five food would have gone rather than two. They arrive at bearing:1 and it changes
nothing, because repeats holds what a firing made aside - so what stops this is the repetition
and never the value, which was measured on 2026-09-20 by setting it the other way and finding the
same four citizens and the same three food.

The two newborns arrive with their bearing already
spent, so they are not parents this turn - and the parents that made them spent theirs in the
making. That is at most doubling, and nothing states it: it falls out of the rule consuming
the citizen it breeds from.

This is what bearing is for, and it is the same shape as hungry one trait along: one stops
a citizen eating all the food, the other stops one breeding with all the surplus. Sean,
2026-09-19: Bearing is their capacity to reproduce.

Fired on its own, so the refreshes do not hide it. In a whole turn the last two steps restore
both traits and every citizen looks alike again; here the world is left as breed left it, which
is the only place the spent bearing can be read.
