Two fed citizens and five food, breeding fired on its own: four citizens and three food.

It stops at two with three food standing, and the food is the evidence. Were the newborns
reached, five food would have gone rather than two. What stops it is repeats, which holds
what a firing made aside - measured on 2026-09-20 by setting the newborns' bearing the other way
and finding the same four citizens and the same three food.

The two parents come back spent and the two newborns arrive whole, which is P-587: breed
puts the parent back at bearing:0 the way toil puts a citizen back at laboring:0, and
everything made arrives at full capability. So the then tells them apart now, and the pair at
bearing:1 is exactly the pair the rule did not reach.

This header said both things before P-587 and the rows said neither. One paragraph had the
newborns arriving spent and another had them arriving at bearing:1; the rows had all four alike,
so what ran was a game where nothing was ever spent and bearing told nothing apart. The
comment described the game Sean meant and the rows described the game there was - which is
spec/README.md rule 3 from the inside: where prose and a test disagree the test is right, and
here the test was right about a thing nobody wanted.

This is what bearing is for, and it is the same shape as hungry one trait along: one stops
a citizen eating all the food, the other stops one breeding with all the surplus. Sean,
2026-09-19: Bearing is their capacity to reproduce. Sean, 2026-09-28: *that is a limiter so that
one citizen can't have multiple children.*

Fired on its own, so the refreshes do not hide it. In a whole turn the last two steps restore
both traits and every citizen looks alike again; here the world is left as breed left it, which
is the only place the spent bearing can be read.
