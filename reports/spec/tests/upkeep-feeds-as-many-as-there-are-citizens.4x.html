Two citizens and ten food, and upkeep stops at two.

The repetition is bounded by whichever runs out first, and here that is the citizens rather
than the food - which is the other half of what
a-citizen-eats-and-one-there-is-no-food-for-starves shows. Eight food is left.

It is fired by name and given no place. upkeep is scoped per territory, so the engine fills
where from every territory there is and a command that named one would be saying something the
rule does not let it say. A test firing it by hand does what the turn does, which is why the
scope is written on the rule.

Nobody perishes, because perish is not part of this. The turn runs the two in order; a test
can run either alone, and this one is about the eating.
