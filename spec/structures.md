## The list

### Extractor

- An extractor produces one resource. Once per turn it may take a unit of labor and produce the
  territory's density for that resource.

### Garrison

- The structure through which the citizens of a territory apply force. A garrison holds force of
  its own, and is what allows units that travel by land to be produced.

### Yard

- A Yard produces Arks.
