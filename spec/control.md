How a game is won and lost. Force, and how territory is claimed and held, are a future plan
in [force](future/force.md); weapons and their ranges are in [combat](combat.md).

## Winning

- A player wins by launching an Ark from a territory other than one an Ark has been deployed to.

## Losing

- A player has lost when they have no citizens and nothing that converts into a citizen, such as
  an Ark or a Pioneer
