The fiction the rules implement. [Invariants](invariants.md) requires that everything in the
game have a cause inside the model rather than happening because the game says so; this is
where those causes are told as a story.

## The player

Each player represents a sentient AI with highly advanced technology. The player can 3D
print life, and endeavors to create life suitable for the environment.

## Life

An Ark prints a planet's founding population, with enough genetic variability for it to be
viable.

The AI designs life generally suited to a particular planet, with enough intellect to
operate its facilities. That suitability is only just enough to survive long enough for
natural selection to figure out the remaining details.

The AI does not have the information or the compute to perfectly tune life to the planet, so
it leaves the remaining work in that regard to biological reproduction.

## The population

The population acts on its own. It operates the machines and works for the AI because of
incentive structures, not because it is commanded.

The AI designs machines and buildings. The population operates them.

## Violence and order

- Violence is inherent. Coordination is imposed.
