# Narrative

- To be merged into the specification and deleted
- Here I tell the story of how I imagine the game loop for a planet progressing
- From this we should be able to figure out a lot of game mechanics

- The ark arrives at a planet, notes natural predators on the planet of violence level 2
- The the ark can deploy to a territory, and transform into a base of operations with security level 3
- The base of operations for a territory can build extractors for food, fuel, and metal
- The base of operations can also manufacture the first organics, the initial population
- The base of operations can manufacture 1 food per turn
- The first order of business is to use the starting 1 population to expend 1 labor build a farm
- Population labor expendure refreshes every turn
- Territories will have zero or more nodes for each resource, each with thier own density.
- The starting territory will have say, 4 nodes at 6 density for food.
- A farm is built, then we generate 6 food
- With 6 food, population doubles, so next turn we have 2 population and expend 2 labor to build 2 more farms
- This brings our total number of farms to 3
- We eventually exploit all resources in a territory
- We can build some kind of pioneer unit to claim neighboring territories like the ark did, these too transform into bases of operations for a territory
- The base of operations also has a logictics network, so can move each resource to an adjacent territory
- Eventually we build a launcher and new ark, and launch our new ark into space
