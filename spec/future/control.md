**These rules are not built and are not abandoned.** They are here because the game wants them
once it can be played, and the first release cannot be played while it is building them.

## Winning

- A player wins by launching an Ark from a territory other than one an Ark has been deployed to.

## Losing

- A player has lost when they have no citizens and nothing that converts into a citizen, such as
  an Ark or a Pioneer
