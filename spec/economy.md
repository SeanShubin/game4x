## Extraction

- A planet's resources never run out; what is finite is the rate at which they can be
  extracted
- The number of nodes determines the number of extractors that can be built for that
  resource on that territory
- The density of each node determines the amount of resources that can be pulled from
  each extractor each turn
- It costs labor to operate extractors
- For example, a territory with three food nodes of densities 8, 6 and 3 supports three
  farms; each farm works one node, and a citizen spending a unit of labor at the farm on
  the density-8 node produces 8 food that turn
